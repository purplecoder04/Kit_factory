SECTION: Lesson 01
TITLE: Repair Starts With Ownership
Repair is not about winning the argument. Repair begins when each person can name their own part clearly.

KEY_TERM: Ownership
KEY_TERM_BODY: The ability to recognize what belongs to you without carrying what belongs to someone else.

CHECK: Name the pattern.
CHECK: Separate blame from responsibility.
CHECK: Choose one healthier next response.

BOTTOM_NOTE: Healthy repair gets specific without getting cruel.
