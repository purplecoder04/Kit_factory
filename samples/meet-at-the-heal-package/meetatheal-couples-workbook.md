TITLE: Welcome
This workbook is for shared application. Each activity is meant to be completed together after both people have had time to reflect.

CHECK: Take turns speaking.
CHECK: Listen without interrupting.
CHECK: Name one shared pattern.
CHECK: Agree on one next practice.

BOTTOM_NOTE: This workbook is not for fixing each other.

TITLE: What We Are Learning Together
